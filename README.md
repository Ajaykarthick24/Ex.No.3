To write, execute, and evaluate prompts using the following Prompt Engineering techniques:

1. Straightforward Prompting

3. Preceding Question Prompting
4. Missing Word Prompting

## SELECTED USE CASE

**AI-BASED HOSPITAL PATIENT MONITORING SYSTEM**

An AI-based hospital patient monitoring system helps healthcare professionals monitor patients' vital signs, identify unusual changes, and provide timely alerts. It uses patient information such as heart rate, oxygen saturation (SpO2), body temperature, and blood pressure to generate understandable monitoring reports.

## EXPLANATION

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing and structuring instructions to obtain accurate, relevant, and meaningful responses from Generative AI tools. Different prompting techniques can be used to obtain information in different formats depending on the requirements of a particular application.

In this experiment, four prompting techniques are applied to the AI-based hospital patient monitoring system to understand how the structure of a prompt influences the generated response.

## PROCEDURE

### 1. STRAIGHTFORWARD PROMPTING

**Definition:**

Straightforward Prompting is a technique in which a clear and direct instruction is provided to an AI model without additional examples or complex explanations. It helps generate quick and relevant responses for simple tasks.

**Prompt:**

"Explain how an AI-based hospital patient monitoring system monitors a patient's health using heart rate, oxygen saturation, body temperature, and blood pressure. Provide the explanation in simple language."

**Expected AI Output:**

An AI-based hospital patient monitoring system continuously observes a patient's vital signs, including heart rate, oxygen saturation, body temperature, and blood pressure. It processes the collected information and identifies unusual changes in the patient's condition. If any measurement crosses a predefined clinical threshold, the system generates an alert for healthcare professionals. This helps doctors and nurses monitor patients efficiently and respond to potential health risks.

**Evaluation:**

The straightforward prompt successfully generates a clear and relevant explanation. It is easy to understand and suitable for obtaining basic information quickly. However, it does not specify a detailed output structure.

### 2. TABULAR FORMAT PROMPTING

**Definition:**

Tabular Format Prompting is a technique in which the AI model is instructed to organize information into rows and columns. It makes information easier to understand, compare, and analyze.

**Prompt:**

"Create a table containing 10 vital health monitoring parameters used in an AI-based hospital patient monitoring system. Include the serial number, parameter name, purpose, example reading, and monitoring action. Present the information in a clear tabular format."

**Expected AI Output:**

| S.No. | Vital Parameter | Purpose | Example Reading | Monitoring Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heart Rate | Measures heartbeats per minute | 75 BPM | Monitor heartbeat variations |
| 2 | SpO2 | Measures oxygen saturation in blood | 88% | Generate an alert for clinical assessment |
| 3 | Body Temperature | Measures body temperature | 37°C | Identify unusual temperature changes |
| 4 | Blood Pressure | Measures pressure in arteries | 120/80 mmHg | Monitor abnormal readings |
| 5 | Respiratory Rate | Measures breaths per minute | 16 breaths/min | Identify breathing abnormalities |
| 6 | Blood Glucose | Measures blood sugar level | 100 mg/dL | Flag unusual glucose readings |
| 7 | ECG | Records electrical activity of the heart | Normal rhythm | Identify possible rhythm abnormalities |
| 8 | Pulse Rate | Measures pulse frequency | 72 BPM | Monitor pulse variations |
| 9 | Body Weight | Tracks changes in body weight | 65 kg | Identify significant weight changes |
| 10 | Sleep Monitoring | Observes sleep duration and patterns | 7 hours | Identify unusual sleep patterns |

**Evaluation:**

The tabular format prompt successfully generates 10 organized health monitoring parameters. It presents the information in a structured manner, making it easier to compare patient measurements and understand their monitoring purposes. This technique is useful for organizing large amounts of healthcare information.

### 3. PRECEDING QUESTION PROMPTING

**Definition:**

Preceding Question Prompting is a technique in which a relevant question is provided before the main instruction. The initial question establishes the context and guides the AI model towards generating a more focused and meaningful response.

**Prompt:**

"Why is continuous patient monitoring important in hospitals? How can changes in vital signs indicate possible health risks? Based on these questions, explain how an AI-based hospital patient monitoring system identifies abnormal readings and alerts healthcare professionals."

**Expected AI Output:**

Continuous patient monitoring is important because a patient's health condition can change unexpectedly. Vital signs such as heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature, and blood pressure provide important information about the patient's health.

An AI-based hospital patient monitoring system analyzes these measurements and compares them with predefined clinical thresholds. When an unusual reading is detected, the system generates an alert and informs the responsible healthcare professionals. This helps medical staff identify potential problems earlier and make appropriate clinical decisions.

**Evaluation:**

The preceding question prompt provides background context before asking the main question. It generates a more meaningful and logically connected explanation. This technique is useful when the AI model needs to understand the reason behind a task before providing an answer.

### 4. MISSING WORD PROMPTING

**Definition:**

Missing Word Prompting is a technique in which one or more words are intentionally left blank in a sentence. The AI model is instructed to complete the missing information based on the given context. It is useful for testing knowledge and generating specific answers.

**Prompt:**

"Complete the following 10 fill-in-the-blank questions related to an AI-based hospital patient monitoring system. Provide the correct missing words."

**Expected AI Output:**

| S.No. | Fill in the Blanks | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The measurement used to identify the percentage of oxygen in the blood is ______. | SpO2 |
| 2 | The number of heartbeats per minute is known as ______. | Heart Rate |
| 3 | The instrument used to measure body temperature is called a ______. | Thermometer |
| 4 | The AI system generates an ______ when abnormal readings are detected. | Alert |
| 5 | The instrument used to measure blood pressure is called a ______. | Sphygmomanometer |
| 6 | The normal adult resting respiratory rate is commonly around ______ breaths per minute. | 12–20 |
| 7 | The electrical activity of the heart is recorded using an ______. | ECG |
| 8 | The measurement of sugar present in the blood is called ______. | Blood Glucose |
| 9 | The AI system analyzes patient data to identify unusual ______. | Patterns |
| 10 | Doctors and nurses use patient monitoring information to make clinical ______. | Decisions |

**Evaluation:**

The missing word prompt successfully generates 10 fill-in-the-blank questions and their corresponding answers. It helps test subject knowledge, identify important healthcare terminology, and evaluate the AI model's ability to complete sentences using contextual information.
